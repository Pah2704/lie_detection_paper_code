# DOLOS Data Access

DOLOS is provided by NTU ROSE Lab for academic, non-commercial research under
the provider's access conditions and Release Agreement. Request access from
the [official dataset page](https://rose1.ntu.edu.sg/dataset/DOLOS/), not this
repository. An access approval does not grant redistribution rights.

This release contains code, configurations and aggregate experimental statistics
only. It does not distribute DOLOS annotations or timestamp/download tables,
training/test membership, original videos, extracted audio or frames, face/flow
caches, checkpoints, clip/window predictions, or access-approval correspondence.
No additional redistribution permission is claimed or sought for this release.

Locally generated tables may retain source IDs, timestamps and ground-truth
labels. They remain restricted even when they are produced by the released code.
Filenames can also reveal Truth/Lie labels. Do not add local `data/` or `outputs/`
directories to a public commit or include them in a shared archive.

The release guard is a preventive check, not a legal certification. Users remain
responsible for their accepted agreement, institutional requirements, source
copyrights and applicable restrictions. The dataset provider does not own the
copyright to the underlying gameshow videos.
